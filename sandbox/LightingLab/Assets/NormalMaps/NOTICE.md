# Normal maps from the Windows App SDK samples

`BeveledEdges.jpg`, `SphericalWithMask.png` and `bubblenm.jpg` are taken
unchanged from the Microsoft sample gallery, where the samples
ThumbnailLighting, LightSpheres and LightInterop use them:

<https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK-Samples/tree/main/Samples/SceneGraph/SampleGalleryApp/Assets/NormalMapsAndMasks>

They are here so that the lighting lab can repeat those samples with the very
maps the samples use.
